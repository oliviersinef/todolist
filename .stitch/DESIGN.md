# Design System: TODOLIST Mobile
**Project ID:** 7072475429635985655

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The atmosphere is **Minimalist, Airy, and Friendly**. The design focuses on "Productivity without stress," using generous white space and soft pastel accents to create a calm user experience. It avoids cluttered lists in favor of clean, card-based layouts.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Pure Zen White (#FFFFFF)**: Background color for all pages to ensure a clean, minimalist look.
- **Pastel Dream Purple (#D1C4E9)**: Secondary accent; used for branding, titles, and non-urgent task tags.
- **Action Orange (#FF9800)**: Primary action color; used for the floating "Add Task" button, "Done" status highlights, and productivity gains.
- **Charcoal Clarity (#2D2D2D)**: Primary text color for high legibility.
- **Muted Lavender (#7E57C2)**: Secondary text and iconography.

## 3. Typography Rules
- **Font**: Inter (Clean, geometric).
- **Headers**: Semi-bold, large spacing (e.g., `headline-lg` for daily greetings).
- **Body**: Regular weight, high line-height to maintain "airiness."

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons**: Generously rounded (pill-shaped or 24px+), no borders, subtle elevation.
* **Cards/Containers**: 20px rounded corners, pure white background on a slightly off-white surface or used with very soft diffused shadows (#000000 at 5% opacity).
* **Inputs/Forms**: Floating labels, 12px rounding, subtle background shift on focus.

## 5. Layout Principles
- **Margins**: Large (24px+) to prevent content from touching edges.
- **Hierarchy**: Vertical flow with clear "Visual Anchors" (e.g., a large productivity widget at the top).
- **Spacing**: Use of white space as a functional tool to separate task categories instead of lines.
